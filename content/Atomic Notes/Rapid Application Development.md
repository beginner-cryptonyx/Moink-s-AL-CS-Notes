### Rapid Application Development
#### Principals
- Parallel development of modules
- Minimal planning
- Flexible development process
- Heavy client involvement, they are involved in all parts of development
- Used for time critical development
- Small incremental releases made, each adding functionallity
- 
-  
#### Benefits
1. **Quicker development** as multiple areas are being worked on simultaneously
2. **Prototype produced in early stages of development**
3. Can accommodate changes in requirements 
4. Easy review possible
5. Progress can be easily measured 
#### Drawbacks
1. **Difficult to estimate time and cost of development**
2. **Documentation often omitted**
3. **Client can change their mind at the end of development**
4. Highly skilled developers required
5. Only suitable for projects that can be modularized

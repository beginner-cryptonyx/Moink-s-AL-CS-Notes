- High definition Multimedia Interface
- Allows transmission of video and audio **in single cable**
- High Definition signals supported
- HDMI offers protection against piracy due to HDCP -> (high-bandwidth digital copy protection)
- fast data transfer rate

- limited cable length to retain quality signal
- non robust connection (easy to disconnect)

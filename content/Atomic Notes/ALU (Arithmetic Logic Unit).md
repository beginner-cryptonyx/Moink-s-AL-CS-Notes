Actions performed by the ALU:
- Performs arithmetic operations (+, -, x, /)
- Performs bit shifting operations
- Performs logical operations (AND, OR, XOR)
- Designed to form integer calculation
- Temporary register Accumulator used when carrying out ALU calculations 

- % some CPU can have 2 ALU's, one for fixed point operations, and one for floating point operations

### Iterative model
#### Principals
#### Benefits
1. Working models developed early in lifecycle, produced at each iteration
2. Easier to test and [[Debugging|debug]]
3. More flexible in terms of changing requirements
4. Client involved during all stages at each iteration
5. Easy to measure progress
6. Better suited for large projects
7. Takes in feedback of customer, **improved product every iteration**
8. Easy to manage risk
#### Drawbacks
1. Design issues if not all requirements are not gathered at the start of the project
2. Requires lot of resources
3. Client could change mind at the end of development

> [!error] WAN
> **Wide Area Network** :: A network connecting computers on different sites, possibly thousands of kilometers apart

### Characteristics
1. Not owned by organization
2. Covers large geographical area 
3. Fiber optic transmission medium
4. Leased from PSTN company who also provide dedicated communication link
5. Low data transmission rate

### Advantages
1. Large area coverage
2. Higher bandwidth
3. Communication of data 

### Disadvantages
1. Maintenance/Troubleshooting difficulty due to size
2. High Installation costs
